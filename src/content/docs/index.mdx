## Why this exists

The same questions get asked in Zambian developer communities every few weeks, get answered from
memory, and the answer scrolls away. "Which payment gateway should I use?" comes up over and over —
and the answers never stick.

Meanwhile the answers rot. A provider that worked in 2024 is down in 2025. A fee quoted once is
repeated for two years after it changed. Someone pastes an AI answer about the wrong company entirely
and nobody notices.

This wiki is an attempt to write those answers down **once**, in public, with dates on them.

## How to read this wiki

Every factual page carries a **verification stamp**: when a human last checked the claim, and
against what. We grade sources as `primary` (the provider's own docs or support),
`reported` (a contributor's own first-hand experience), or `hearsay` (repeated in community
discussion, never re-checked).

**Treat `hearsay` as a lead, not a fact.** Contributions that haven't been independently verified
are marked hearsay. Upgrading them is the single most useful thing you can do here. See [how we verify claims](/verification/).

## What this wiki is not

- **Not legal or financial advice.** Licensing and tax pages describe what practitioners report.
  They are not a substitute for a lawyer or the regulator's own guidance.
- **Not a vendor directory.** Providers and their staff are welcome to correct facts about their
  own products, [with disclosure](/contributing/#if-you-work-for-a-provider). Marketing copy gets reverted.
- **Not a support channel.** If a provider's API is down, this wiki can tell you whether that is
  normal. It cannot fix it.
