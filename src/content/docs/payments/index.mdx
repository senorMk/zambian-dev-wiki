This is the most common question in Zambian developer communities.

## The short answer

**First decide whether you need cards, because that changes everything.**

| You need | Start with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mobile money only** (most local products) | PawaPay, 543 Konse Konse (cGrate), Lipila | Cheapest rates, simplest onboarding, no card compliance burden |
| **Cards as well** | DPO | The most widely used option for card acceptance. See [card payments](/payments/card-payments/) — options are limited. |
| **International customers** | A foreign entity + Stripe, or a merchant-of-record | See [receiving international payments](/payments/receiving-international-payments/) |
| **Recurring subscriptions on mobile money** | Nothing does this | See [below](#subscriptions-do-not-work-on-mobile-money) |

## Do not pay a setup fee without asking why

Setup fees in this market range from **zero to K12,000** for functionally similar integrations,
and the fee often disappears if you do the integration yourself.

| Provider | Setup fee | Date reported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lipila (Hobbiton) | Free | Apr 2026 |
| 543 Konse Konse / cGrate | Free if you self-integrate; $1,000 and K5,800 also quoted | Sep 2025 / Oct 2023 |
| DPO | None; free test environment | Oct 2023 / Feb 2025 |
| Digital PayGo | None if you self-integrate | Apr 2026 |
| Tingg (Cellulant) | $500 or $1,000 (varies) | Oct 2023 / Mar 2024 |
| Kazang | $2,000 commitment fee | Oct 2023 |
| ZynlePay | K7,000 | Apr 2026 |
| PrimePay / PrimeNet | K12,000 | Aug 2025 |

Aggregators are not themselves charged a once-off integration fee by MTN, Airtel or Zamtel, so a
setup fee represents a margin rather than a pass-through cost.

<Aside type="note">
**Contested.** DPO's setup fee has been reported as ~K5,000, but multiple sources contradict
this and indicate no setup fee is charged. The weight of evidence favours no fee.
</Aside>

## Transaction rates

Directionally accurate; **verify before you build a margin on any of these.**

| Provider | Rate | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cGrate / 543 Konse Konse | 1.5% mobile money | Aug 2025, Apr 2026 |
| Digital PayGo | 1.5% | Apr 2026 |
| Tilt (Zoona) | 2.5% + K1,000/month minimum commission | Dec 2023 |
| eLipa | ~2.5% (pre-launch) | Oct 2023 |
| Direct MNO integration | ~2% | Mar 2025 |
| PrimePay / PrimeNet | 3% mobile, 4% card | Aug 2025 |
| Tumeny | 3% or 4% (contested) | Mar 2024 |
| Lenco / BroadPay | 3.5% mobile, 3.8% card | Dec 2023 / Mar 2024 |
| Tingg (Cellulant) | ~5% | Mar 2024 |

Going direct to each mobile network is cheaper per transaction but requires managing three
integrations, three reconciliations and three floats. A single aggregator merchant account is
generally worth the spread unless volume is high.

## Settlement — ask before you integrate

| Provider | Settlement terms | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lenco / BroadPay | Instant on mobile money, T+1 local cards, T+8 international | Jul 2024 |
| Sparco (pre-Lenco) | Daily to bank above ~K1,000 | Oct 2023 |
| DPO | T+2, auto-settles above ~$100 balance | Oct 2024 / Oct 2023 |

DPO settlement is a common operational pain point. Withdrawing collected funds can be slow,
with delays of a month or more reported in some cases.

## Subscriptions do not work on mobile money

**Zambian mobile money has no card-style recurring debit.** You cannot pull funds on a schedule.
There is no auto-debit or standing-order mechanism available through mobile money.

Common workarounds:

- **Prompt-and-remind** — notify the user, they approve each cycle. What most local products do.
- **A wallet you top up** — the user funds a balance you draw down. Adds float and, depending on how
  you hold the money, potentially a regulatory obligation. See [regulation](#do-you-need-a-boz-licence).
- **Card debit orders** — works, but you need card acceptance and possibly PCI DSS compliance.
- **A merchant-of-record for foreign customers** — a common approach for SaaS products.

## Do you need a BOZ licence?

**Usually not, if you use somebody else's rails.** A Bank of Zambia licence is required if you
**hold customer funds** or want to connect to the National Financial Switch. If you are calling
an existing gateway's API and the money sits with the mobile network or the aggregator, you
generally do not need one.

<Aside type="danger" title="Do not rely on this wiki for licensing">
Cost and duration estimates range widely, from less than K5,000 to $70,000 or more, and from
three months to three years. These figures are unreconciled and likely reflect different licence
categories.

Get requirements from the Bank of Zambia directly — their payment systems department publishes
designation requirements and accepts meeting requests.
</Aside>
